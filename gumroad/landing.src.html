Halo Pro macOS · Apple Silicon · 2.3 beta

Halation, the way
film makes it.

A Mac app that adds film halation to your photos in 32-bit float. Light scatters back the way it does through film: a tight red-orange core with a soft tail, and blue light makes no glow.

Free for personal use. Pro for paid work.

The glow

Not a blur with a red tint. Light that comes back.

Built from the film up

Light that gets through the emulsion reflects off the back of the film base and comes back red. Halo Pro follows that light: a tight core, a soft tail, red-orange where it's strong. Blue light makes no glow, as on film.

A film look to start from

It opens on a subtle red rim around bright edges with a gentle warmth on bright surfaces, tuned against measured reference looks. Push it from there.

Float, start to finish

Every step runs in 32-bit float. No banding in 8- or 16-bit exports, 32-bit TIFFs keep highlights brighter than white, and the preview matches the export.

The controls

Film terms, not filter sliders.

  • Amount, SizeHow strong, how wide. Size goes up to 16, where the glow lands as an even veil.
  • FormatThe film size, from Super 8 to 4×5. The halo belongs to the film, so it's relatively bigger on small formats.
  • Roll-offFrom a tight film bell to a long, dreamy tail.
  • Starts atHow bright something must be before it glows, in stops below white, with a soft start.
  • Green returnHow much green comes back, turning the hottest cores orange.
  • LandingHow much glow shows over surfaces that are already bright.
  • Warmth on highlightsThe gentle warmth on bright surfaces. 0 for the glow alone.
  • Glow colorAny colour for the glow, for looks no film makes. It keeps its shape and brightness.

Also: Mist for a diffusion-filter softness, fringing, lens smear, grain, one LUT, and masks to keep the glow where you want it.

Good to know

Mac

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple Silicon. Notarized by Apple.

Files

Opens TIFF (8, 16 and 32-bit), PNG and JPEG. Exports TIFF up to 32-bit float, PNG and JPEG, with your colour profile and metadata.

Beta

2.3 is a beta. Very fine diagonal stripes can show faint bands in the glow, and very big glows can land unevenly. If something looks wrong, send it over.
